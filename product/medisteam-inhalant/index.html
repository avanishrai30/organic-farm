Product Overview
Details

MediSteam Inhalant is your go-to natural remedy when seasonal colds, allergies, or flu symptoms start affecting your comfort. Specially formulated with pure menthol vapors and soothing eucalyptus oils, this over-the-counter rub delivers fast, effective relief from nasal congestion, chest tightness, and persistent coughing — without the found in many traditional inhalants.

Whether you're suffering from nighttime coughing fits or need support to breathe more freely during the day, MediSteam provides comforting vapors that open up the sinuses, soothe inflamed airways, and promote relaxation. Perfect for both adults and children aged 2 and up, this topical rub is a must-have addition to every American home medicine cabinet.

Ingredients
  • Menthol (5%) – Natural cooling agent to open airways.
  • Eucalyptus Oil – Known for its anti-inflammatory and expectorant properties.
  • Camphor (4%) – A traditional decongestant used for soothing chest rubs.
  • Petrolatum & Beeswax Base – Skin-friendly and long-lasting vapor release.
How It Works

MediSteam uses a powerful, yet natural blend of menthol, eucalyptus, and camphor vapors to:

  • Open congested nasal passages.
  • Relieve chest pressure and promote easy breathing.
  • Calm coughing reflexes, especially at night.
  • Support restful sleep and respiratory comfort.
Usage Instructions
  • Apply a small amount to the chest, throat, or back up to 3 times daily.
  • For steam inhalation, add a teaspoon of MediSteam to a bowl of hot (not boiling) water.
  • Inhale the soothing vapors for 10–15 minutes with a towel over your head.
  • For external use only. Do not apply to broken skin or ingest.

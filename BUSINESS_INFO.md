## 4. Special Initiatives & Programs

### 🐄 Adopt a Cow (Gau Seva Mission)
- **Concept**: Protect and nurture indigenous cow breeds (such as Gir cows) while preserving traditional Indian agricultural heritage.
- **Participation Options**:
  - Adopt a cow with monthly/yearly support.
  - Sponsoring cow feed, medical care, and shelter.
  - Farm visits to meet adopted cows.

### 📞 Emergency Cow Rescue Service
- **Function**: Community service to report injured, sick, or distressed cows for immediate rescue.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Spotter reports cow details and live location via form/call.
  2. Rescue team dispatches immediately.
  3. Medical aid, shelter, and long-term care provided at VC Organic Farms.
- **Emergency Rescue Hotline**: +91 7892397382

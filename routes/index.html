✳ Cold Chain Standards ✳

Farm-fresh integrity
from milking to doorstep.

STANDARD 01 Strict 4°C Chill

Active cooling

Milk is chilled within 45 minutes of milking and maintained under 4°C throughout transit to prevent bacterial growth without preservatives.

STANDARD 02 Zero Plastic

Sanitized glass

Packaged in food-grade, hot-water steam sterilized glass bottles that preserve flavor without harmful microplastics or phthalate leaching.

STANDARD 03 Silent Drop

Peaceful mornings

Trained delivery captains drop bottles into insulated doorstep thermal bags quietly before 7:00 AM, protecting your family's morning sleep.

STANDARD 04 Fresh Batch Daily

Never stored

We do not maintain warehouse inventories. What is milked at dawn is delivered at dawn; any surplus is converted into fresh paneer or curd.
